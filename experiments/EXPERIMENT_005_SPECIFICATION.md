# Experiment 005 Specification: Self-Supervised Embodied World Model & Dynamic Brain Growth

**Project**: 3D-Brain

**Phase**: Phase 5  
**Date**: September 2026  
**Status**: APPROVED & SPECIFIED  
**Author**: 3D-Brain Research Group  

---

## 1. Scientific Objective

The central research question guiding Experiment 005 is:
> **Can a spatial recurrent neural substrate learn a predictive internal model of an environment through self-supervised sensorimotor interaction, while dynamically modifying its own structural connectivity?**

Experiment 005 evaluates whether self-supervised prediction, active embodiment, and dynamic structural plasticity provide measurable computational advantages over frozen, passive, and static-topology baselines across stationary and non-stationary environments.

---

## 2. Experimental Design Matrix

The experiment systematically explores five controlled experimental dimensions:

### Dimension 1: Learning Mechanism
1. `FROZEN`: Frozen baseline control ($\Delta W = 0$).
2. `LOCAL_PRED`: Local predictive coding using feedback alignment and Widrow-Hoff readout updates.
3. `NEURO_PRED`: Three-factor neuromodulated predictive learning gated by global prediction error magnitude $M(t) = \tanh(\beta \|e_t\|)$.
4. `GRADIENT_PRED`: Online gradient descent / backpropagation control condition.

### Dimension 2: Embodiment Mode
1. `ACTIVE`: Closed sensorimotor loop where internal neural dynamics drive action selection $a_t \sim \pi(h_t)$, altering future environmental states and sensations.
2. `PASSIVE`: Open loop where the agent passively observes sensory streams generated by an external exploration policy without active control.

### Dimension 3: Structural Topology & Growth
1. `FIXED_TOPOLOGY`: Static spatial topology (Phase 4 baseline). Connections and neurons are immutable.
2. `DYNAMIC_SYNAPSES`: Dynamic synaptic plasticity under constant neuron count ($N = \text{const}$). Synaptogenesis is guided by spatial proximity and co-activity; low-weight/inactive edges are pruned subject to degree bounds $[k_{\min}, k_{\max}]$.
3. `DYNAMIC_GROWTH`: Dynamic synapses + controlled neuron recruitment (Dynamic-N) triggered by persistent high prediction error ($\mathcal{E} \ge \theta_{\text{grow}}$).

### Dimension 4: Environmental Regimes
1. `STATIONARY`: Constant 2D arena layout (Environment A) across 300 steps.
2. `ABRUPT_SHIFT`: Non-stationary shift: Environment A (100 steps) $\to$ Environment B (100 steps) $\to$ Environment A (100 steps). Evaluates surprise spike, adaptation rate, catastrophic forgetting, and recovery.
3. `RECURRING`: Rapid cyclical alternation between Environment A and B every 40 steps.
4. `GRADUAL_DRIFT`: Landmark coordinates drift continuously over time ($\Delta \mathbf{p} = 0.01$ per step).
5. `NOVEL_EVAL`: Zero-shot held-out evaluation on a completely unseen arena geometry with 6 beacons.

### Dimension 5: Scale Ladder
Evaluated across controlled population sizes:
* $N = 40$ (standard experimental resolution)
* $N = 100$
* $N = 300$
* Scaling verification ladder up to $N = 1{,}000$ and $N = 10{,}000$ units.

---

## 3. Baselines & Causal Control Conditions

To isolate causal mechanisms, Experiment 005 enforces the following baseline comparisons:
* **Baseline 1 (Frozen Spatial RNN)**: Evaluates whether recurrent echo dynamics alone yield predictive information without weight modification.
* **Baseline 2 (Passive Observer)**: Evaluates whether active motor selection provides representational advantages over passive observation.
* **Baseline 3 (Predictive Learning without Structural Plasticity)**: Evaluates synaptic weight adaptation over a fixed graph.
* **Baseline 4 (Predictive Learning + Structural Plasticity)**: Evaluates joint synaptic and topological adaptation.
* **Baseline 5 (Ablations)**:
  * Ablation A: Remove prediction error ($e_t \to 0$ in learning rules).
  * Ablation B: Remove structural rewiring (freeze adjacency mask).
  * Ablation C: Remove spatial distance penalty ($P_{\text{form}} \propto 1$).
  * Ablation D: Disable neuron growth (Fixed-N).

## 4. Quantitative Metrics

### 4.1 Predictive Accuracy
* **Next-State Prediction MSE**:
  $$\text{MSE}_{\text{next}} = \frac{1}{S} \sum_{k=1}^S (s_{t+1, k} - \hat{s}_{t+1, k})^2$$
* **Multi-Step Prediction Error**: Autoregressive rollout error over horizon $H \in \{1, 2, 3, 5\}$.
* **Action-Conditioned Contrast**: Predictive divergence $\Delta \hat{s} = \|\hat{s}(a_1) - \hat{s}(a_2)\|$ under distinct motor commands.
* **Adaptation Rate**: Number of steps post-shift required to return prediction error to criterion ($\text{MSE} \le 0.08$).
* **Retention & Forgetting**: Prediction error on Environment A before vs. after exposure to Environment B.

### 4.2 Representational Metrics
* **Linear Probe Decoding**: $R^2$ accuracy of linear decoders trained on internal state $h_t$ to predict:
  1. Agent metric coordinates $(x_t, y_t)$.
  2. Latent environmental hidden cycle $z_t$.

### 4.3 Structural & Network Metrics
* **Edge Count Evolution**: $E(t)$ over time.
* **Average Degree**: $\langle k \rangle(t)$.
* **Structural Modularity $Q_{\text{struct}}$**: Newman modularity of the physical synaptic adjacency matrix.
* **Functional Modularity $Q_{\text{func}}$**: Modularity of the cross-correlation matrix of neural activations.
* **Connection Distance Distribution**: Mean Euclidean length of newly formed vs. pruned connections.
