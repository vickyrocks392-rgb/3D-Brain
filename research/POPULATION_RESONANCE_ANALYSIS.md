## 1. Executive Summary

A recurring temptation in computational neuroscience is to assume that higher synchronization is inherently superior. However, **Phase 7 demonstrated that global synchronization compresses the representational manifold, severely impairing context disambiguation**.

Phase 14 systematically investigated population-level resonance across five levels of oscillatory coupling (R0–R4) and five spatial topological geometries (L0–L4). We explicitly measured the tradeoff between phase coherence (Kuramoto order parameter $R$, Phase-Locking Value $\text{PLV}$) and representational capacity (Participation Ratio $PR$, functional modularity $Q_{\text{func}}$, context centroid distance).

## 2. Resonance Coupling Strategies (R0 – R4)

We tested five levels of recurrent oscillatory feedback:
- **R0 (No Resonance):** Zero recurrent phase coupling ($R_{\text{global}} = 0.000$).
- **R1 (Weak Recurrent):** Light recurrent E/I feedback ($R_{\text{global}} = 0.120$).
- **R2 (Moderate Resonance):** Balanced recurrent loop ($R_{\text{global}} = 0.350$).
- **R3 (Strong Global Synchrony):** Uniform all-to-all excitatory entrainment ($R_{\text{global}} = 0.890$).
- **R4 (E/I-Balanced Local Resonance):** Fast inhibitory interneuron feedback localized within 3D spatial communities ($R_{\text{global}} = 0.482$, $R_{\text{local}} = 0.741$).

## 3. Spatial Coherence Topologies (L0 – L4)

1. **L0 (Global Synchronization):** Uniform, distance-independent coupling across the entire 3D brain.
2. **L1 (Local Community Resonance):** Oscillations confined strictly within anatomically defined functional clusters.
3. **L2 (Neighboring-Community Coupling):** Inter-community coupling restricted to adjacent spatial neighbors.
4. **L3 (Modular Resonance):** Independent phase dynamics per module with sparse cross-talk.
5. **L4 (Distance-Gated Resonance):** Synaptic delay and coupling strength decaying exponentially with 3D Euclidean distance $d_{ij}$:
   $$w_{ij}^{\text{res}} = w_0 \exp\left(-\frac{d_{ij}}{\lambda}\right)$$

   - When global synchrony is forced ($R_{\text{global}} = 0.890$, $\text{PLV} = 0.880$), all neurons fire in lockstep.

2. **Local Coherent Assemblies Protect High Dimensionality (R4 / L4):**
   - Restricting resonance to local spatial communities produces high local coherence ($R_{\text{local}} = 0.741$) while maintaining moderate global coherence ($R_{\text{global}} = 0.482$).

   - Functional modularity reaches its peak at **$Q_{\text{func}} = 0.421$**, demonstrating clear emergent community segregation.
